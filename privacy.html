BROKE privacy

Last updated October 8, 2026

BROKE is a pay-yourself-first app. It has no server and no accounts of its own. We don’t collect, sell or see your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (payments, splits, savings, who owes you) is stored in your browser on your device. If you clear the app’s site data, it’s gone unless you made a backup or connected Google Sheets.

Google Sheets sync (optional)

If you connect Google, BROKE asks for two things:

  • Your email address, shown in the app so you know which account is connected.
  • Access to files BROKE creates (the drive.file permission). BROKE creates one spreadsheet called “BROKE” in your own Google Drive and reads and writes only that file. It cannot see your other files.

Your data travels directly between your device and Google. It never passes through any BROKE server, because there isn’t one. The Google access token is kept on your device and expires after about an hour.

You can disconnect any time in the app (Splits → Google Sheets → Disconnect), and remove BROKE’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The spreadsheet stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Use of Google data

BROKE’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to show and sync your own records in the app.
